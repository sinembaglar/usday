Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

UsDay is an app that lets couples, families and friends keep their plans, special days and surprises in a shared calendar. This policy explains which data is processed when you use UsDay, why, and what your rights are. It is written with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, No. 6698) in mind.

Who is responsible for your data

Sinem Bağlar (the data controller)
Contact: sinembglr@gmail.com

What data we process

  • Account details: your name, email address, password and birthday (day and month only; we never ask for the year or store your age). Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • What you add: plans (title, date, time, place, category, note), surprise hints, the tickets and photos you upload, your cover photo, your own templates, comments on plans, emoji reactions and the GIFs you pick, your wish list and the wishes you said you’re getting, and your answers to group plans.
  • Notification details: if you turn on notifications, the technical address needed to reach your device (a browser subscription or your phone’s notification token).
  • Usage details: when you last opened the app (to count what’s new), your language and time zone (so notifications and reminders arrive in your language and at your local time), and, if you use it, your personal calendar subscription link. We also keep when you last opened a plan’s chat; the people who can read that chat see “Seen” under the last comment.
  • Subscription details: the type of your UsDay+ subscription, its start and end dates, whether you’re on a free trial and who it covers. Apple or Google handle your card and payment details; we never see them.
  • Feedback and reports: the message you send and, with feedback, the type of device you wrote from; with a report, who you reported and why. The reported person is not told.
  • Error logs: if something breaks in the app, the technical error text, the page it happened on, your device and browser type, the app version and, if you’re signed in, your account. They are used only to find and fix bugs and are deleted after 30 days. The contents of your plans, comments or photos are never part of them.
  • Kept on your device: your session, and your theme, language and calendar view settings. These stay on your device.

We do not collect your location, your contacts or data from other apps. We do not use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • To create your account, let you sign in and reset your password; to show your plans to the people you connect with and keep surprises hidden until their reveal day; to send notifications and reminders; to add your plans to Apple or Google Calendar if you ask; and to check your subscription and who it covers. This is necessary to provide UsDay to you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), performance of a contract).
  • To keep UsDay safe and working: error logs, handling reports and blocking accounts that break our Terms. This is in our legitimate interest in running a secure service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Phone notifications are sent only after you allow them on your device. You can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings.

We don’t use your data for any other purpose.

Who can see your data

  • A plan is seen only by the people on the calendar it was added to: each connection (your partner, a sibling, a friend) has its own calendar, and nobody sees the others’ plans. Group plans are seen only by the people you invite. The details of a plan you mark as a surprise stay hidden from the person it’s for until the reveal day you choose.
  • People you share your calendar with (Settings → Share my calendar) can see either just the times of your other plans or their times and names, as you choose. Places, notes, photos and who you’re with are never shared, and the name of a surprise never shows. These people can write about your plans: what is written by people who see your plan names is seen by you and everyone who sees your plan names; what is written by people who see only busy/free times, and anything about surprises, is seen only by the two of you. The other people on the plan don’t see these chats. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • The people you’re connected with see your wish list. When someone says they’re getting one of your wishes, you don’t see it; your other connections do, so nobody buys the same gift twice.
  • The people you’re connected with, and people you share a plan with, see the day and month of your birthday. Your connections get reminders 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before it. These reminders are never sent to you.
  • Other users can’t see your data.
  • The app’s team can see overall numbers (how many users and plans, for example), your name, email address, the dates you joined and last signed in, and the feedback and reports you send, in order to run the service. The admin panel never shows the contents of your plans, surprises or messages.
  • We work with the service providers below to run UsDay. They process data only on our behalf and only to provide the service:
    • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in (servers in Frankfurt, Germany)
    • Vercel: hosting the website
    • Resend: sending emails such as password resets
    • Apple and Google notification services: delivering notifications to your device
    • RevenueCat: checking App Store and Google Play subscriptions. Only your account number (a random ID) and your purchase record are shared; your name and email are not.
    • KLIPY: GIF search in comments. Only the word you search for is sent; your name, email and IP address are not, as searches go through our own server.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area and Türkiye, for example in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA, it is protected by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard offered by the provider.

If you downloaded UsDay from the App Store or Google Play, the download and your payments are covered by Apple’s or Google’s own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, your account and the plans, photos, templates and notification records you added are deleted right away. They may stay in technical backups for a short while longer. Plans added by the people you’re connected with stay in their accounts.

How to delete your account: usday.app/delete-account

Security

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules in the database make sure each person can reach only the data of their own connections. Photos are stored in a private space, not on the open web.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). To use any of these rights, write to sinembglr@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Age

UsDay is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

When we update this policy, we change the date on this page. We also announce important changes in the app.
