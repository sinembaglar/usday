Deleting your UsDay account

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In the app

  1. Open UsDay and sign in.
  2. Tap ⚙️ Settings at the top right of the cover photo.
  3. Tap Delete my account permanently.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

If you have a UsDay+ subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it separately in the Subscriptions section of your App Store or Google Play account settings.

If you can’t get into the app

From the email address you signed up with, send an email with the subject “UsDay account deletion” to sinembglr@gmail.com. Your account is deleted within 30 days at the latest, and we’ll let you know.

What is deleted and what stays

  • Deleted: your account (name, email, password), the plans and notes you added, the tickets and photos you uploaded, your own templates, your notification records and your calendar subscription link.
  • Stays: plans added by the people you’re connected with stay in their accounts. If nobody else is left on a shared calendar, everything on it is deleted, including the shared cover photo.
  • Data may stay in technical backups for a short while, and is then deleted for good.
