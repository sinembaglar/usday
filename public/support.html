Support

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to us in the app from ⚙️ Settings → Send feedback; your message comes straight to us.

Can’t get into the app? Send an email to sinembglr@gmail.com. We reply as soon as we can.

Getting started

How do I add someone to my calendar?
  1. On the home screen, tap Add person and choose who they are (partner, sibling, friend…).
  2. Send the invite link by message.
  3. When they open the link and sign in, your shared calendar is ready.

You get a separate calendar with each person; nobody sees the others’ plans.

How do I plan a surprise?

When adding a plan, turn on Surprise mode, write a hint and pick the reveal day. Until then, the person it’s for only sees the hint; the plan’s name, place and notes stay hidden.

What does “I’m getting this” on a wish list do?

It marks that you’ll get that wish. The wish’s owner never sees it, but your other connections do, so nobody buys the same gift twice.

Can I see my plans in my phone’s calendar?

Yes. In ⚙️ Settings → Your phone’s calendar, add it to Apple Calendar or copy the link for Google Calendar. Surprises stay hidden there too.

Subscription

Does the person I invite have to pay too?

No. One UsDay+ subscription covers you and 5 people you’re connected with; they don’t pay separately.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → UsDay.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → UsDay.

If you cancel before the free trial ends, you won’t be charged. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current period.

I changed phones and my subscription doesn’t show.

Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple or Google account, then tap ⚙️ Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases in the app.

Are my plans deleted if my subscription ends?

No. Everything stays where it is and your calendar becomes read-only. Subscribe again and you pick up where you left off.

Can I get a refund?

Apple or Google take the payment, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com, or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

Account and safety

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password; a reset link comes to your email.

Notifications don’t arrive.

Check that notifications for UsDay are on in your phone’s settings (iPhone: Settings → Notifications → UsDay; Android: Settings → Apps → UsDay → Notifications). If it still doesn’t work, write to us.

I want to block or report someone.

In ⚙️ Settings → People, open the person and tap Report. The person isn’t told, and you can block them at the same time.

How do I delete my account?

⚙️ Settings → Delete my account permanently. Details, and what to do if you can’t get into the app: usday.app/delete-account
